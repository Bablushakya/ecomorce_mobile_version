Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the laddoos sweetened? ▼

We use zero white refined sugar. All sweetness comes naturally from sun-ripened Arabian black dates and unrefined organic desi jaggery.

What is the shelf life of the sweets? ▼

Because we use 100% pure desi ghee and roasted grains with zero water content, our laddoos stay fresh for up to 30 days in an airtight jar at room temperature.

Do you deliver across India? ▼

Yes! Every order is freshly hand-rolled and dispatched within 24 hours via express courier partners with door-to-door tracking.
